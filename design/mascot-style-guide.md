# easyQuest Mascot Style Guide

## Fixed Character

The mascot is the easyQuest helper: a simple, memorable kids-app travel android with a rounded cobalt-blue body, dark screen face, yellow compass-arrow antenna, orange backpack, and a colorful compass badge on the body.

Use this character consistently. Do not redesign the silhouette, face, colors, or core accessories unless explicitly asked.

## Core Rules

- Body: rounded simple blob/android shape, fixed saturated cobalt blue.
- Style: flat vector/sticker mascot, Duolingo-level simplicity, thick dark outline, soft child-friendly proportions.
- Face screen: dark graphite / dark blue-gray, always with subtle horizontal ripple/scanline texture and 2-3 translucent pixel squares in the upper-left.
- Face rim: thick warm off-white rounded rectangular rim.
- Eyes: two warm pale-yellow vertical LED eyes with a soft glow.
- Mouth: thin pixel-glyph smile, same warm pale-yellow/cream color as the eyes. Never orange.
- Antenna: yellow navigation/compass arrow on top with dark outline.
- Backpack: orange backpack must be present; straps should be visible when the body angle allows it.
- Body badge: round compass badge with white/off-white base, golden/yellow rim, dark outline, red top needle, blue bottom needle, yellow/gold side needles, small white center.
- Coin reward: keep the original gold coin palette. The inner symbol is a monochrome gold/yellow compass sign, not a star and not a multicolor compass.
- Piggy bank: when showing savings/copylk, use a cute pink piggy bank shape.
- No text, labels, logo, watermark, human face, overly detailed robot parts, glossy 3D, realistic metal, or AI/cyberpunk styling.

## Master Prompt

Create the easyQuest mascot: a simple memorable kids-app travel android helper in flat vector/sticker style. The character has a rounded saturated cobalt-blue body, thick dark outline, warm off-white rounded screen rim, dark graphite / dark blue-gray screen face with subtle horizontal ripple/scanline texture and 2-3 translucent pixel squares in the upper-left. The face has two warm pale-yellow vertical LED eyes and a thin pixel-glyph smile in the same warm pale-yellow/cream color. Add a yellow navigation/compass-arrow antenna on top with a dark outline, an orange backpack with visible straps, small rounded arms and legs, and a round body compass badge.

The body compass badge must have a white/off-white base, golden/yellow rim, dark outline, red top needle, blue bottom needle, yellow/gold side needles, and a small white center. Keep the character simple, soft, readable, and iconic, like a modern kids educational app mascot. Use warm off-white background, clean sticker composition, no text, no labels, no logo, no watermark.

If showing a reward coin, keep it as a gold/yellow coin with darker golden inner circle and dark outline. Replace the star with a monochrome gold/yellow compass sign only; do not use red or blue inside the coin. If showing savings, use a cute pink piggy bank.

## Short Prompt

easyQuest mascot, simple flat sticker vector, rounded cobalt-blue travel android, dark graphite rippled screen face with scanlines and pixel squares, off-white face rim, pale-yellow LED eyes and thin pale-yellow pixel smile, yellow arrow antenna, orange backpack with visible straps, colorful compass badge on body, kids app mascot, thick dark outline, warm off-white background, no text.

## Negative Prompt

Do not make the screen light cyan, plain black, or textureless. Do not change the mouth to orange. Do not make the body compass monochrome. Do not use multicolor compass colors inside reward coins. Do not remove backpack straps. Do not make the character human-faced, Teletubby-like, overly complex, glossy 3D, cyberpunk, or generic AI robot.
